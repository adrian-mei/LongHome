# Code of conduct

LongHome contributors are expected to make participation safe, respectful, and
welcoming. The project deals with grief, death, families, identities, and irreplaceable
personal material; empathy is part of technical quality.

## Our standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment include:

- showing empathy and respect for differing experiences and cultures;
- giving and accepting constructive technical feedback;
- using inclusive, grief-sensitive language;
- respecting privacy and consent; and
- taking responsibility for mistakes and repairing harm.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, sexualized attention,
threats, deliberate disclosure of private information, trolling, and sustained disruption
of project spaces.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions, comments, issues, and other participation
that violates these standards. They may temporarily or permanently restrict participation
when necessary to protect the community.

Report conduct concerns privately through the repository owner's GitHub profile. Reports
will be reviewed as confidentially and promptly as practical. Maintainers will avoid
conflicts of interest when handling a report.

## Scope

This code applies in project spaces and when someone is publicly representing LongHome.
